# Locations

Entries marked SYNC\_PATH. The outposts follow the register in Protocol 001. Grid references are given as the register gives them. The register sorts ground into four classes, A to D, which Protocol 001 defines.

## The Citadel

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Reference | SYNPTCL/LOC/015 |
| Name | The Citadel |
| Category | SYNC\_PATH / TRIGGER\_V1 |
| Summary | The occupier's command centre, in the north of the city. |
| Entry | The nineteenth-century fortress on the Vistula in the north of the city. Command centre. It controls river access from the north. Colonel Viktor Nikolaevich Sorokin, the commanding officer, is based here. Protocol 002 records that no excesses are attributed to him, and that a source who delivered food to his office in July found the desk clear but for a single paperback and a glass of water.
