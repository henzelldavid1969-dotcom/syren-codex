# Story source

The Story view is built from one file: `manuscript.md`.

To refresh the manuscript, replace `manuscript.md` with the new version and
commit it. The site rebuilds on its own. Keep one `## ` heading per section,
with headings that match the chapter table (reference/ENTRY_PROTOCOL.md,
section 8); `supabase/load_chapters.py` checks them.

Current text: the contracted final of 8 November 2026 (contractions in
first-person narration; Interludes I and VI and the Protocol chapters 3, 6,
9, 12 and 15 uncontracted). The text is final and is not edited here.

The other two files are separate on purpose:
- `back-cover.md`: the introduction on the Story page.
- `appendix.md`: Appendix: The Brief, shown after the Acknowledgments.
